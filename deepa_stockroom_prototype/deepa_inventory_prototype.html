How to count: Enter full bottles as a whole number and loose spirit as the measured remaining ml. Loose ml must be from 0 to below the pack size for the selected SKU. For spirits, inferred sales = opening volume + received volume − closing volume − wastage; divide the result by 60 ml to show peg-equivalents. Beer and sparkling wine are shown in bottle-equivalents, not pegs. A negative result means the counts need review.

Product catalogue

Each line is a brand and fixed pack-size SKU. Add separate entries for the same brand bought in different sizes.

Fixed pack-size rules: Spirits: 375, 500, 750 or 1,000 ml. Beer: 500 or 650 ml. Sparkling wine: 330 ml. Pack sizes cannot be typed freely, reducing the risk of mismatched bottle-volume calculations.
